## What the observation channels can and cannot establish

**Measured.** Scalar force and heat-transport diagnostics expose different aspects of the state. Exact repeats do not imply equality across randomized domains. Cylinder pointwise phase differences coexist with very similar mean drag. RBC-medium/hard mean Nu and native volume kinetic energy both separate ic-1 from the other domains, with matching spatial differences.

**Inference.** Cylinder representations should retain phase information, while RBC representations may need large-scale spatial organization in addition to bulk heat transport. A scalar reward alone can conceal such differences.

**Unresolved.** The runner checked benchmark observations but did not save their per-step sensor tensors; the saved public fields are resampled. Exact policy-observation reconstruction is not guaranteed. Future representation training must preserve the official observation operator and avoid pretending these fields are the original observations. Likewise, sparse snapshots every 20 steps are not a full-state checkpoint or high-rate vorticity series.
