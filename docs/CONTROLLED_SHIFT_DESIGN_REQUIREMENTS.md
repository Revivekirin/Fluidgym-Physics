# Controlled-shift design requirements

This is design comparison only. No intervention is selected, implemented or executed. Official FluidGym source, approved matrix and rewards are unchanged. Future RL implementation belongs in `Fluidgym-RL`.

Evidence labels: **Measured/source-traced** identifies existing data/code; **Inference/design requirement** is a proposed condition for scientific interpretability; **Unresolved** states what this nominal campaign cannot decide.

## Common requirements before any physical intervention

**Measured.** The nominal campaign establishes repeated zero-action trajectories, a supported easy Cylinder shedding candidate, and initialization-dependent RBC-medium/hard heat/energy/spatial behavior. It contains no nonzero-action response measurements. **Inference/design requirement:** these observations support baseline diagnostics and representation tests, not actuator identification, planner performance or robustness to changed physics.

A future intervention must have one explicit independent control variable and a causal definition: preserve geometry, grid, timestep policy, solver tolerances, observation operator, action protocol and metric normalization unless that quantity is the declared intervention. Record effective values after every reset and load. Distinguish a sudden parameter quench from separately equilibrated initial states; their transient responses answer different questions. Existing initial domains must not be silently relabeled or regenerated.

Numerical changes are validation controls, not physical interventions. A separately reviewed convergence study may be necessary, but no such change is made here. Require relevant native residuals, divergence/mass-balance and thermal/force checks; record accepted internal dt sums independently of the accumulated float32 clock. Use independent initialization controls and fixed diagnostic definitions. Do not confuse a different reward baseline with changed physical performance.

**Inference/design requirement.** In the nominal nondimensional RBC formulation, changing Ra at fixed Pr changes both nu and kappa. With a separate buoyancy amplitude A, effective Ra scales with A/(nu*kappa) for fixed thermal/length scales and Pr with nu/kappa. A change to only one component must be described by its effective groups, not its constructor label. Likewise inlet-speed and actuator gain changes must not be misrepresented as clean viscosity interventions.

## Prioritized connection to representation and TD-MPC studies

1. **Priority 1 — Nominal representation evaluation, no new physical shift.** Use phase-aware rollout diagnostics for Cylinder: raw force error, mean drag and validated spectral/phase behavior should be reported separately so a phase offset is not mistaken for changed mean dynamics. Use RBC Nu, native kinetic energy and spatial temperature/velocity organization together. Split by initial-domain identity; keep repeated seeds and benchmark/extended prefixes in the same split. Confirm the official observation tensors are available before claiming policy-observation learning from resampled fields.
2. **Priority 2 — Close measurement gaps before causal conclusions.** Plan independent accepted-dt accumulation, saved native vorticity/residual/heat-balance diagnostics, and provenance for the observation/actuator state. Analyze existing saved data first; do not automatically spend another nominal campaign merely to repeat these means. If RBC persistence is the research question, a separately reviewed matched-initialization/longer-horizon study should target the observed topology separation rather than declare attractors now.
3. **Priority 3 — Identify action-conditioned nominal dynamics.** Propose bounded, reproducible excitation sequences and held-out initial-domain evaluations. Zero-action trajectories do not constrain the response to TD-MPC action proposals. Cylinder smoothing state and RBC action normalization must be represented or controlled in those designs. No excitation is run here.
4. **Priority 4 — Compare controlled intervention protocols.** For each candidate in the table, specify effective parameters, state preparation, numerical acceptance checks and observable hypotheses before selecting one. Test representation generalization separately from planning adaptation; evaluate multi-step physical prediction and closed-loop return separately. Architecture, latent objective, algorithm and shift method selection remain open and belong to subsequent approved work.

**Unresolved.** The present data cannot establish which intervention best challenges a latent world model, whether a representation is Markovian under sparse benchmark observations, or whether TD-MPC outperforms another controller. The strongest current evidence is for reproducible nominal scalar dynamics, phase-sensitive Cylinder trajectories and persistent RBC initialization structure—not physical-shift robustness.

**Measured/source-traced.** RBC reward is reference Nu minus observed Nu. The ic-1 low-Nu response occurs under the same zero-action policy, so initialization alone can change return. **Inference/design requirement:** compare future controllers on matched domain identities and report raw Nu/energy alongside reward; do not attribute this baseline difference to learned control.
